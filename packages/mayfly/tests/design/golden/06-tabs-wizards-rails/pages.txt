page 3/4 (Ctrl+N): a vertical rail: the content follows the cursor live; ↑/↓ move, → or Enter
enters the content. ← leaves one level at a time, innermost first (see below)
focus levels, outer to inner: rail → content control → a field inside it (a select, a picker)
← moves out exactly one level. A control that uses ← itself (a select adjusts, a tree row
collapses, a text field moves its caret) keeps it;
the first ← it does not use goes to the rail, wherever the rail sits. Shift+Tab and Esc always
step out one level too, and the hint row shows ← labels whenever ← goes to the rail.
